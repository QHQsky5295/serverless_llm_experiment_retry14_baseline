## Workload Fit

Sarathi-Serve is a scheduling-system baseline centered on chunked prefill and
stall-free scheduling. It can be useful related work for LLM inference
scheduling, but it is not ready to consume the closed PrimeLoRA workload:

- no PEFT/LoRA adapter loader;
- no LoRA-aware model executor layers;
- no per-request adapter identity in the serving path;
- no `e2e_v3` bridge for our 500-adapter true-remote replay.

Treating each adapter as a merged full model would alter the workload and is
not a fair LoRA comparison.

## Decision

Sarathi-Serve remains appendix/gate evidence only. Do not promote it to a
formal row unless an upstream-compatible path can replay the unchanged
Llama-2 7B and Llama-3.2 3B true-remote LoRA workload without adding a new
LoRA serving system to Sarathi-Serve.
